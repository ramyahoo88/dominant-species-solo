# Response to GPT's v0.5.5 re-audit (4874b18)

Release: v0.5.6. The re-audit passed the original Ice Age fix and the printed components, and found two Tooth & Claw decision cases (F1, F2) and two short card reminders (P3). v0.5.6 fixes all three. It also adds the user's cards-only mode for The Red Queen and the rulebook clarifications from NotebookLM's reading that GPT checked.

**F1: human Mammals can win with fewer VP.** Agreed. The forecast took the save with the most VP for a human Mammals player. It now tries every legal save and takes the one that best helps Mammals win: final place first (ties to the food chain, as final standings do), then Mammals' lead over the best other animal, then Mammals' VP, then the save the Extinction chooser would make. On your save (`source/fixtures/gpt-v055-human-mammals-can-win.json`) the forecast is now Mammals 17, Birds 16 (the Savannah save), and Birds take Fertile. With `--paper-options v055Forecast` it is again 18–19 and Birds take Ice Age.

**F2: Birds take Ice Age on a losing tie.** Agreed. The Ice Age value now uses the projected winner after the food-chain tie-break: a tie the rival would lose counts as a loss. On your save (`gpt-v055-birds-losing-tie.json`) Birds forecast 18–18, see Mammals win the tie, and take Fertile. A constructed variant checks that one more VP for Birds turns it back into Ice Age.

**Rulebook clarifications (your check of NotebookLM's points).** All adopted, with no change to play:
- Added elements: your wording, in the terms (rulebook) and on aid 4. It matches the app: removals take the leftmost copy (`discardElement`), and new elements and Metamorphosis replacements go at the right end.
- Block the leader: it only claims the space. Its target is any unscored tile with the rival's species, and in Execution the pawn scores by the usual Domination rule (rulebook step 3, aid 1).
- Speciation target: "a disk matching the element of the space being checked is on Earth" (rulebook, aid 1); "earth" is now "Earth".
- Migration: the conditions are checked again before each move, so once a species has arrived on a tile, a later move there must be a rescue (rulebook, aid 2).
- Competition: "Keep half-points exactly; do not round." (rulebook, aid 2, Arachnids' aid).
- The Dominance list stays at 26 cards; no Forest Fire.

**Cards-only mode (new).** A third setup choice, "The Red Queen · cards only". The app shuffles and draws each rival's cards (with the same shuffle and draw code as full games), shows each drawn card, reshuffles a deck's discards when it runs out and says so, and offers Undo, Export and Import. The rulebook and the ten player aids are built into the app (`source/paper/rq_embed.js`, generated by `printables.py`; a test checks it matches the printables) and open offline in a sandboxed frame. It tracks no board, so it saves under its own key and format, and a test checks that a cards-only save and a full-game save are never read as each other. `source/browser_draw_test.py` runs it at 390 px, offline.
